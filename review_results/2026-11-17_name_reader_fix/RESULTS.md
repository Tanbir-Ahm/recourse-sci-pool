# The name reader fix (2026-11-17)

Owner: "Build the name reader fix" (after the investigation of 2026-11-16, `review_results/2026-11-16_retrieval_miss/RESULTS.md`). Research branch, nothing deployed. **No answer model was involved anywhere.** All questions, target cases and readings are Claude's, not a lawyer's. Say "no hallucination rate has been measured".

## In plain terms
The authority step boosts a judgment that many other judgments name. Its name reader could not recognise a case written with "Ltd.", "Private Limited", "Corpn." or "and Others", so company-named cases (the cheque-bounce and commercial-offence cases) looked as if nobody had cited them. The reader is fixed and both tables are rebuilt.
- **Counts:** on 11 company-named cases I chose before looking (none of them the diagnosis cases), the table credited 75 of 263 mentions before and 239 of 263 after; eight of the eleven were credited 0 times, none is now. On a no-hand-picking sample of company-named cases the share of mentions credited went from 6% to 26%; plain-named cases did not get worse (19% to 21%).
- **Search:** on 34 new questions (17 cases, one lay and one lawyer wording each, none naming the case) the expected case was among the first 8 judgments for 7 with the step off, 21 with the old table (what staging runs) and **24 with the new table**; company-class 10 to 14 of 20, ordinary names 11 to 10 of 14. Three questions of 34 is not a proof.
- **Your section 141 case:** S.M.S. Pharmaceuticals (2005) was credited by 2 judgments and is now credited by 74; it moved from places 8, 24, none to **1, 1, 1**. Gunmala Sales was credited by none and is now credited by 15 (the text names it in 15), **but it is still not in the 8 the site feeds: places 12, 11, 10** (12, 20, 13 before; 6, 5, 5 with the step off). HDFC Bank (2025) and Aneeta Hada (2008) moved down (24, 14, 10 to 26, 22, 14; 10, 7, 7 to 31, none, 21). So the reader was one of two causes: the boost is also weak for a case named by only 15 judgments. Not fixed.
- **Wrong links:** reading new links against the sentence that made them, 3 of 57 were wrong in round one (two causes fixed), **1 of 53 in the final version** (about 2%); the 40 old links I read had 0 wrong of 33 readable. Of each sample of 60 (40 for the old links), 3 to 7 (7 for the old ones) could not be located in the text, so they are unchecked.
- **Lost:** 628 of the 39,704 old links (1.6%) are no longer found. Of 30 I read, about half were old links that pointed at a different case with the same party names (a mistake the old table made), the rest real ones (mostly short names such as "Rama and Ors", whose key is now under 5 letters; not all traced).

## What was wrong (diagnosis of 2026-11-16) and what changed in `authority_graph.py`
1. A dotted word of 4 or more characters ended a sentence, so "S.M.S. Pharmaceuticals Ltd. v. Neeta Bhalla" returned nothing. Now the word right before "v." never ends a sentence and a list of dotted abbreviations (Ltd, Pvt, Co, Corp, Corpn, Inc, Bros, Govt, ...) is not a sentence end inside a name.
2. "Limited" against "Ltd", "Private" against "Pvt.", and no suffix at all were different names. Now corporate-form words at the END of a party (ltd, limited, pvt, private, co, company, corp, corporation, corpn, inc, llp) are dropped on both sides of every comparison (never the whole name; "Life Insurance Corporation of India" keeps its middle word).
3. "and Others" left a stray "and" ("anumehtaand"). A trailing "and" is dropped.
4. A leading "in" (from the sentence) was already handled by trying shorter starts; it only failed because of 2.
Round two, after reading 60 new links: "Mahesh s/o Ram Narain and Others vs. State" was read as "Ram Narain" and linked to a case named Narain (the reader now keeps "s/o", "d/o", "w/o", "alias", "@" inside the party so the party is cut there); and a trailing "Research Centre" fragment of "Heart Institute and Research Centre" linked a Modern Dental College case to it (descriptive words such as research, centre, college, hospital, school, medical, dental, engineering, technology, foundation, association, education are now treated as shared words).
Tried and REVERTED: a rule that the second party of a mention must share a distinctive word with the case's own before a first-party-only match is allowed. It stopped "Hindustan Construction Co. v. State of Bihar" being linked to "... v. Gopal Krishna Sengupta", but removed about 900 more real links (the index writes "U.P." and "State of U.P." where the case is "Uttar Pradesh", and some names in the index are garbled), so it was a net loss. Its numbers are kept in the files named `reverted_overlap_rule_*`.
Tests: `test_authority_graph.py` 38 checks (9 new); `test_sci_search_service.py` passes. The tables `authority_table/authority_d8811a9.json.gz` and `authority_3c80a26.json.gz` were rebuilt (about 3 minutes each, free); a rebuild with the final code gives tables equal to the measured ones.

## Method (the order matters)
Committed before any run: the first target list `targets_v1.py` (37 cases chosen from memory; 25 found in the index; 12 not held and dropped), the second list `targets_v2.py` (never used to change the reader), the sample protocol `sample_eval.py` (fixed seed 20261117: 150 company-named and 150 plain-named judgments drawn from the names table, a full-text query built from the names alone), and the 34 end-to-end questions `endtoend_questions_v1.json`. Baselines were counted with the OLD reader and table before the reader was touched (`baseline_*`, `sample_baseline_new.json`). The ground truth for "how many judgments name this case" is a full-text search of the index, which is independent of the reader but has its own blind spots (it does not match "Corpn."; Indian Oil Corporation shows 126 credited against 71 text mentions for that reason; I read 14 of the extra links: 13 clearly real, one unclear).

## What this does not show
- Nothing has been tried with the answer model, on Railway, or by a lawyer. The step is OFF in production (`SCI_AUTHORITY` unset) and its staging setting is the owner's.
- More links means more boosting of anything the courts name, **including judgments that were later overruled or departed from**. The register's line still attaches where a case is listed; a case not on the register gets no warning. Example: Anil Hada v. Indian Acrylic (2000) is, on my recollection, a decision Aneeta Hada (2012) departed from; it is now credited by 14 judgments. A count of "named by" is not a measure of good law.
- Remaining reader gaps seen while measuring, NOT fixed: "Ch." and similar honorifics before a name (State of Haryana v. Ch. Bhajan Lal: 77 credited against hundreds of text mentions, many of them bare "Bhajan Lal"), a parenthesised second party ("State (NCT of Delhi)": Shiv Kumar Jatia stays at 0 credited), garbled party names in the index's own names table, two different cases merged into one cluster when the first party is a State or the Union and the second party's letters agree ("State of Rajasthan v. Mohan Lal" and "Union of India v. Mohanlal"), and the 641 old links no longer found (628 if only the case, not its record number, is compared).
- The end-to-end test is 34 questions on 17 cases: a difference of three questions is within what chance and my choice of cases can produce.
- The second list was never used to change the reader, but it holds only one company-named case, so it adds little.
