Owner's go: "Motor Vehicles Act. Put it on the Act shelf, so the answer can state its sections." Reason: in the staging test of a truck driver who hit a man and drove on, the answer could state nothing under the Motor Vehicles Act ("its text is not in the material"). Code: Recourse branch `register-hooks-and-overlay`, NOT on `main`. Nothing tried with the answer model; nobody has read the text; `text_status` is `machine_checked`.
