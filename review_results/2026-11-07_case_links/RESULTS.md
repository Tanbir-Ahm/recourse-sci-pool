# Judgments page: the title-index card had no link to the judgment (owner, 2026-11-07, screenshot of "KESHAVAN MADHAVA MENON versus THE STATE OF BOMBAY": "no link to the citation judgement shown. We have embedded the court links to supreme court and even high court before")

## Cause
The Judgments page shows a title-index card (Supreme Court, 38,026 judgments, name + date + citation only) for a name or citation search. That index's `source_url` column is empty for every row, and the card builder gave it only an Indian Kanoon *search* link (which the page showed below the "Not checked by us." line). The court links the owner remembers live in the names table of the WIDE index (`link`, `link_kind`) and travel with wider-search passages; the title index was never joined to them.

## Fix (Recourse branch `register-hooks-and-overlay`; no front-end change needed, the page already renders a card's `links`)
`build_case_links.py` (offline, a person runs it) writes `case_pdf_links.json.gz` (0.33 MB): for each title-index row the PDF's key in the open Supreme Court bucket (CC-BY-4.0, Dattam Labs) and, where the wide index's names table has the court's own address for the same judgment, that address. `case_links.py` looks a case id up at run time; `search_service` puts "Read the judgment (Supreme Court website)" (court's own) or "Read the judgment (PDF copy)" (bucket) first on an index card and keeps the Indian Kanoon search link after it. A library judgment keeps its own link. `CASE_LINKS=off` is the way back.
## Coverage and how a row is tied (nothing guessed)
38,016 of 38,026 title rows (99.97%) have a bucket PDF; 4,035 (10.6%) also the court's own address. A row is tied only when the bucket's metadata has the same case id (or, for 126 rows whose id is not an INSC number, the same citation) AND the same title (letters and digits) AND the same decision date, all such rows name ONE path, and the bucket's own key listing (43,572 English PDFs, listed year by year) holds that path (the path is listed under two year folders for 5,200 judgments: the decision-year folder is taken). Not linked: 6 (an id naming two paths), 4 (no key listed). The official address needs the same normalised name and date and exactly one address.
## Checked
- 60 random bucket links and 25 random court addresses fetched (first 1 KB): all 85 serve `application/pdf` (the court's files begin with a 4-byte wrapper before `%PDF`, as they do for the wider search's links); 12 bucket PDFs and 8 court PDFs read in full: the first two pages carry the case's party names for all 20 (three flagged by a crude word test were checked by eye: title typos only).
- The owner's exact query now returns the card with "Read the judgment (PDF copy)" first.
- Tests: `test_case_links.py` (27 checks), `test_search_service.py` (new section 11, ALL PASSED), Recourse suite 136 of 136.
## Limits
A link opens a document; it does not say that the document is the right authority or that the law is unchanged. The bucket copy is a public mirror, not the court's website (the CC-BY credit is the page footer's). The links were built from the title index as it stands (to 12 Dec 2025); a judgment added later needs `extend_index_from_bucket.py` and a rebuild (`build_case_links.py --cache DIR --names-table ...`). The High Court tab was not touched (its results come from the wider search and already carry their links). `case_document.py` ("Source link") still reads the empty `source_url`; not changed.

## Addendum 2026-11-07 (owner: "By this information, around 9000+ should have a direct supreme court link. The old crawl + new archive, check and tell me")
The owner is right about the wide index: its names table holds **9,352 Supreme Court judgments with the court's own address** (8,113 old crawl + 1,239 newer archive). The first build tied only **4,035** of them to a title-index card, because it matched the two lists by exact name and date and the names differ in spelling (the header-parsed name against the bucket's typed title: "Satwant"/"SATW ANT", "Mohanlal"/"MOBANLAL").
Rebuilt with three guarded passes (`match_official`): (1) the same Supreme Court Reports citation, a date within 400 days and parties that read alike, loosely (985); (2) the same date and parties that read alike, the first party above all (4,381); (3) a near-identical name where the header's date was misread (567). **Title-index cards with the court's own address: 5,847** (was 4,035). Bucket PDF links: 38,016 of 38,026, unchanged.
**Why not 9,352: 3,347 of the court-linked judgments have NO row in the title index to attach a link to** (318 are dated after the index ends on 12 Dec 2025, about 312 have no date, the rest are judgments the title index does not list at all: it is the list of reported cases, the wide index also holds unreported ones, e.g. "Moijullah v. State of Rajasthan" 2003); 21 fitted two title rows and 41 title rows were claimed by two different court files, and those get none. Those judgments are reachable on the Judgments page only through the wider search (by words), not by name or citation: a name search over the names table would be a new feature (not built, not approved).
**Errors found and fixed on the way:** a sample of 94 PDFs showed two wrong links (two judgments begin on the same Reports page, [1968] 2 S.C.R. 408: State of Gujarat v. Jaswantlal Nathalal and Munshi Ram v. Delhi Administration; and one same-date, same-respondent pair, Sodagar Singh v. State of Punjab / Teja Singh v. State of Punjab and Others); the matcher now needs the parties to read alike in every pass and the first party decides. After the fix 120 of 120 sampled court PDFs (weighted to citation matches and to links whose date differs) carry the title row's first party; the 4 weakest matched pairs have first-party similarity 0.79 to 0.84 and are spelling variants of one name ("Pathumma & Anr" / "PATHUMMA").
