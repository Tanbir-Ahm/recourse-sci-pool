This looks like a real legal question, but it is outside what Recourse checks — it covers police arrests, FIRs, criminal procedure and bail under the BNS and BNSS.
TADA (Terrorist and Disruptive Activities Prevention Act) is a specialized counter-terrorism statute outside BNS/BNSS scope; sedition convictions and appeals in TADA courts are not covered by this chat feature's BNS/BNSS arrest and police procedure focus.
For this kind of question, speak with a lawyer who handles that area of law.
