# How the search found the passages, and whether the register was used (answered 2026-10-09; PRIVATE)

## 1. The three ways of finding passages (versions A, B, C, E, F)

`review_tools/pool_runner.py`, `Engine.retrieve`, which copies the first pack's pipeline: (a) **meaning**: the question is turned into a voyage-law-2 fingerprint and the 100 nearest of the 236,155 passages are taken; (b) **keyword**: a word search (SQLite FTS5, ranked by BM25) on the first 12 distinct words of four letters or more that are not common words, top 50; (c) **section number**: for every number that follows "section", "sections", "s." (or looks like 498A, 143A), a phrase search for "section N", up to 30 per number. The three lists are merged by reciprocal rank fusion (K = 60) into 60 candidates; `rerank-2` rereads the question with each candidate; the score is multiplied by the passage-type weight and, in version A, by 1.10 if the passage names the section the question mentions. Version S is the Recourse site's own code (`semantic_retrieval.py`, read-only): meaning search (top 50, cosine at least 0.40, at most 4 judgments within 0.08 of the best) plus at most 2 keyword extras; its exact section lookup is off here (no statute tables in the index).

**Measured on this run** (`retrieval_paths/retrieval_paths.py`, 31 query embeddings, about $0.0003): of the 95 passages in version A's top 5 over the 19 questions, 88 were in the meaning list, 42 in the keyword list and 11 in the section list; 38 were found by two or three ways, 50 by meaning only, 5 by keyword only, 2 by section only, none by none. A section number was found in 9 of the 19 questions as worded, and in all 12 blind rewrites (they name provisions); in the rewrite arm 23 of the 60 top-5 passages were in the section list. 25 of the 45 top-5 passages of A for the 9 questions with a number name one of those sections.

Limits: the section search needs the word "section" (the story R12 says "420 and 406 IPC", so none was found); it matches the number, not the Act; the keyword search uses only the first 12 usable words, so a long story is searched by its opening (R22: younger, brother, convicted, killing, people, 2019, village, land, fight, sessions, court, gave: nothing about the death sentence).

## 2. The Overruled Register was not used

No search, scoring or pack code mentions the register (`grep -i "goodlaw\|register"` over `pool_runner.py`, `real30_score.py`, `build_real30_pack.py`, `real30_build.py` finds nothing); the run record (`pool.json`, `run`) has no register field; the plan (section 6) says "the register is NOT on while marking" so that relevance numbers stay clean. `Overruled_Register_Full_Review.xlsx` is a review copy for people; no code reads it. After the search, the only currency list used was a separate one of 16 older rulings (`real30_stale_status_v1.json`), to count how many reached the top 5.

**What the register would have done** (`currency_pass_on_this_pool.json`, offline, no cost): stale-ruling passages in the top 5 and how many it would have badged: A 4 and 1, B 4 and 1, E 7 and 4, S 5 and 2, C 3 and 0, F 4 and 0.
