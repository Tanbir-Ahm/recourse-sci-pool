# Addendum for Mr. Advocate: Sheet B, item 1 (Q15, Shivangi Bansal) (PRIVATE; send with the workbook)

The owner's item 1 asks whether Shivangi Bansal (two judges, 2025) endorses Family Welfare Committees that Social Action Forum (three judges, 2018) had disapproved. We have now read the judgment itself (*Shivangi Bansal v. Sahib Bansal*, T.P.(C) 2367/2023 and connected matters, 22 July 2025, 2025 INSC 883, non-reportable; CJI B.R. Gavai and Augustine George Masih J.; Indian Kanoon page https://indiankanoon.org/doc/41565735/).

What we read (our reading, not legal advice):
- It is a consent settlement of a matrimonial dispute (custody, quashing of every pending case, apology, land transfer, undertakings), with the marriage dissolved under Article 142 (para 25).
- The only reference to Family Welfare Committees is the last paragraph, para 26: "The transfer petitions and special leave petitions are disposed of in terms of the above order. The guidelines framed by the High Court of Allahabad in the impugned judgment dated 13.06.2022 in Criminal Revision No. 1126 of 2022 vide paras 32 to 38, with regard to 'Constitution of Family Welfare Committees for safeguards regarding misuse of Section 498A, IPC shall remain in effect and be implemented by the appropriate authorities."
- No reasons; Social Action Forum and Rajesh Sharma are not mentioned; the two-month cooling period and the bar on arrest are not in the Supreme Court's text (they are in the High Court's guidelines it cites by paragraph).

Questions for you: (1) Does a one-sentence direction of this kind, in a non-reportable consent order under Article 142, have any precedential weight against the three-judge Social Action Forum? (2) How should a research tool describe it to a user: as a ruling, as an order in a settlement, or not at all? (3) Has any later bench or High Court treated it as authority?
