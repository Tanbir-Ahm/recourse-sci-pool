# Three fixes after the Muslim Women Act staging test (2026-11-12)

Owner: "Build 1, 2 and 3". Recourse branch `register-hooks-and-overlay`, NOT on `main`. Nothing tried with the answer model; every question here is Claude's, not a lawyer's. Evidence in the owner's log (`CHAT_TURN` turn 2 `rewrite: used`, then `ANCHORS handed over: trial_delay_...`; `GROUNDING_CHECK ... "name": "7(c Rahna Jalal v. State of Kerala)", "status": "not_found"`).

## 1. Case-name reader (grounding_checks.py)
Cause: in "(Section 7(c) (Rahna Jalal v. State of Kerala)" the reader read "7(c" backwards into the name, so a real, sourced case was reported "not found ... do not rely on it". Fixes: a section reference (7(c), 35(3), 482(1)(a)) is never part of a name; a name does not reach back across a bracket that is still open at its start; an unbalanced closing bracket is stripped from the end of a name; the closing test for a name's own bracket ignores a trailing ; , . (this one made "(Prabir ... State (NCT of Delhi); Vihaan Kumar v State of Haryana)" one name); a bracket that holds a court, a bench, a year or a citation is not a name's bracket.
Measured on 1,127 distinct paragraphs of the saved answers (the owner's staging conversations): 401 mentions before, 401 after; 20 paragraphs changed, every change a clean-up (trailing ")" removed 12 times, "State" gaining "(NCT of Delhi)" 4 times, a year / court bracket dropped 4 times). A first version lost "Hussainara Khatoon (I)" and "State (NCT of Delhi)" in the first party; the comparison caught it before it was kept.

## 2. Court cell of a library judgment (authorities_table.py, answer_export.py)
Cause: library rows carried no court and the export defaulted an empty cell to "Supreme Court of India" (Sri Manjunath M P is a Karnataka High Court judgment, 2026:KHC:2726). Fix: the row's court is read from its own record (`corpus/` and `pilot_corpus/`; a pilot record with no court whose source is `api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/` is the Supreme Court's); the Court column appears when a library or wider-search row is not a Supreme Court one (a Constitution Bench of the Supreme Court counts as the Supreme Court); an unrecorded court says "not recorded", never "Supreme Court of India". Of the 68 library judgments 58 have a recorded court (47 Supreme Court, 2 Constitution Bench, 9 other courts) and **10 have none on record** (Pravat Chandra Mohanty, Mathai Verghese, Lalita Kumari, Satish Chander Ahuja, S. Vanitha, D. Velusamy, Hiral Harsora, Deepa v Vijayalakshmi, Indra Sarma, Prabha Tyagi): they say "not recorded" when the column is shown; the owner or a reviewer can add the court to their corpus records. Not done: the answer TEXT does not name the court of a library judgment.

## 3. Age is not the length of a trial (excerpt_anchors.py)
Cause: `_TRIAL_YEARS` reads "case ... 31-year" as a case that has run 31 years; after the owner's follow-up was rewritten the rewrite carried "31-year-old" next to "case"/"proceedings", and the two seven-judge-bench excerpts (P. Ramachandra Rao, Hussainara Khatoon) were handed to a compounding question. `ANCHORS` is on in production: the same happened to any question giving a client's age next to "case". Fix: ages ("31-year-old", "aged 45 years", "age of 55", "19 years of age") are removed before the test. I could not see the rewrite text; the mechanism reproduces on the owner's two questions with a plausible rewrite.
Measured on a set of 24 age-not-delay and 10 age-and-delay questions written and committed (`852c736`) before the fix: false fires 19 of 24 -> 0; delay questions kept 10 of 10. Regression over 1,411 distinct question strings in the research repo: fires 44 -> 25, and the only 19 that changed are age questions (listed in the run); no other question changed.
