## Build 1: follow-up retrieval (what worked and what did not)
**Cause found for "s.185 was not shown in the follow-up":** `carry_forward.refs_from_turns` took the first three named sections in the order they were handed over (s.203, s.205, s.204), so s.185, the section the whole answer turned on, was left out. **Fixed (default, every conversation):** the sections of an earlier answer are ranked by how often the answer names them, the opening (Bottom line) counting double, and up to four are carried (was three). Tested with the real scenario's shape (`test_carry_forward.py`).
**Cause of "sections 3 and 181 never reached the follow-up":** the search by meaning. Section 181 reads "Driving vehicles in contravention of section 3 or section 4" and never says "licence"; no sentence about a licence is near it. Two general remedies were built and MEASURED, and **neither helped, so neither is shipped on:**
- Sentence-level search (`SHELF_MULTIQUERY`, default OFF): each sentence of a long message searched on its own, up to two extra sections. On 21 multi-sentence questions written and committed before it (`eval_questions_multisentence.json`; facts then asks, 6 Acts): 19 of 21 handed over the expected section without it, 19 of 21 with it (the same two misses, MS01 s.181 and MS07 s.166). For the staging follow-up it added ss.35 and 15, not 3 or 181. A sentence's best score does not separate a relevant sentence from an irrelevant one (0.29 to 0.40 for both).
- Embedding the short sections that cite other sections together with the cited sections' text (65 sections): on the five gold sets (75 + 32 + 24 + 30 + 21 questions) top-5 unchanged in every set (71, 29, 23, 28, 19); not built into the loader.
**What this means:** the miss on s.181 is NOT fixed. What is done about its effect is the prompt clause below (the answer must not argue from what it cannot see), and the honest gap line it already wrote.
**ABSENCE_RULE (default on, every answer):** a prompt clause outside the Hard rules: what the material does not show is a gap to report, never a ground or a conclusion; "a ground that depends on a provision you cannot see is not offered as a ground". Before: the follow-up listed as a ground "no Motor Vehicles Act section in the material penalises mere non-renewal", when ss.3 and 181 do. Not tried with the answer model.

## Build 2: Part II of the BNSS First Schedule for offences under other laws (`OTHER_LAW_CLASS`, default ON)
`offence_class.other_law_block`: for every section of a law other than the BNS, BNSS, BSA and the IT Act (which classifies its own offences, s.77B) that reaches the answer and names a punishment, the prompt gets the imprisonment terms found in the section's own text by code (brackets and asterisks of India Code removed), the Part II row they fall in (extracted from the Gazette PDF, `machine_extracted`, one source, nobody has read it), and the instruction to say it as the Schedule's default and that the Act's own provisions (a special court, a power of arrest without warrant, a section such as PMLA 45) govern where they speak. Lines use the Act's display name. Worked on: MV Act ss.185, 187, 181, 189 (under three years: non-cognizable, bailable, any Magistrate); POCSO s.4 (up to life: Court of Session); PC Act s.7 (three to seven years); not listed: monetary-penalty sections (MV ss.194, 196), duty and disqualification sections, sections with no maximum term. Limits: s.185 is classified by Part II as non-cognizable and bailable although MV Act s.203(3) lets the police arrest without warrant (the block tells the model that the Act's own power governs); the rule reads the highest maximum in the text, so a section whose first and later offences fall in different bands is flagged for the model to apply per limb. `test_other_law_class.py`.

## Build 3: two checks on the finished answer (`section_checks.py`, `SECTION_CHECKS`, follows `GROUNDING_CHECKS`: log, or a notice in warn)
- Section gloss: a sentence that begins "Section N (of the Act) <verb>" and uses TWO or more words that the section's text lacks but one other section of the material has (words of the person's question, discourse words such as "supports", and words in more than 4% of the shelf's sections are left out). Finds the staging sentence "Section 182(1) ... obtains a licence by fraud or misrepresentation" (both words are s.19(1)(e)'s).
- Argument from absence: a sentence before "Gaps and checks" that says the material has no X and concludes from it (", so ...", "should not be treated"); a sentence that only reports the gap ("cannot be stated") is left alone. Finds the staging ground.
**Measured on the 10 saved answers I hold (all development material: the rules were shaped on them):** first form 16 false alarms in 16; after "begins Section N verb", two words from the same section and the discourse list: 1 alarm, which was my reconstruction of the material missing a word of the real BNSS 483 text; both staging defects found; nothing else flagged. **Recall is low by design** (one borrowed word, or a gloss in a sentence that does not begin with the section, is not found) and there is no held-out set. A notice says where the words are and that absence from our sources does not show absence from the law; it never says what the law is. `test_section_checks.py`; `acts/section_checks_eval.py` reruns the check on exported conversations.
